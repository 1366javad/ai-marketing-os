```md
# ADR-001

# Campaign-Centric Architecture

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

AI Marketing OS can be built in two ways:

1. Tool-Centric
2. Campaign-Centric

---

## Decision

We choose Campaign-Centric Architecture.

Campaigns are the primary object of the system.

Tools operate around campaigns.

---

## Tool Mode

Standalone generation.

No campaign required.

---

## Campaign Mode

Campaign attached.

Uses Campaign Context and Campaign Memory.

---

## Consequences

Research, SEO, Content, Creative, Ads and Analytics become connected through shared campaign memory.

This creates a long-term competitive advantage through context accumulation.

---

## Rejected Alternative

Tool-Centric Architecture
```
